## Concepts

- **ReadyPass:** Helps students check document and registration readiness
  before travelling to campus.
- **Gate-to-Room Pass:** Guides students from campus check-in through
  registration and hostel room handover.

The `.fig` file is a backup of the design at the time it was uploaded.
The Figma links point to the current design and prototype.

The prototype simulates interactions; example data and integrations shown
in the screens may not represent live services.

## Current stage

Initial wireframes, clickable prototype flows, a testing-plan template,
and a space for future improvements have been prepared. User testing
and evidence-based improvements should be recorded when completed.
